Response Time & Support Policy

We review community inquiries and emails regularly. Most correspondence is addressed within 24 to 48 business hours.

When reporting a technical problem, sharing the following details helps us resolve it quickly:

  • Your device type and operating system (e.g., iPhone 13, Windows 11, Android).
  • Your browser and version (e.g., Safari 17, Chrome 129).
  • A brief description of what happened and steps to reproduce.
