Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

Welcome to XO Tactix (available at https://xotactix.me). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our tactical puzzle game.

1. Information We Collect

XO Tactix is designed as a client-first, privacy-respecting browser game:

  • Gameplay & Progress Data: Your level progress in Puzzle Quest, Daily Challenge streak, unlocked skins, sound settings, and game statistics are stored locally on your device via localStorage. This data never leaves your browser unless you use cloud sync features.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not require account registration, passwords, email addresses, or phone numbers to play the game.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep the game free for players, we may partner with third-party advertising and analytics networks (such as Google AdSense, Google AdMob, or game platform SDKs):

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on prior visits to our website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage solely to save your in-game preferences, sound toggle, and completed puzzle levels. We do not use tracking cookies for cross-site behavioral profiling without your knowledge.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

XO Tactix is a family-friendly game suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the European Union). If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without parental consent, we take immediate steps to delete that data.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

Since your game progress is stored in your device's browser local storage, you have complete control over your data at all times. You can delete all stored game progress simply by clearing your browser cache and cookies, or via the in-game Reset data option.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our practices, please contact us at:

Email: support@xotactix.me / GitHub Project Issues
